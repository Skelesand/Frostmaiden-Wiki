## Events
### Ear to the Ground
[[Avarice]] found Acorn in [[Bryn Shander]] and offered her a deal. Keep an ear to the ground for her and she will pay handsomely. Hazelpuff accepted the stone bat's ear she was given but has expressed no interest in truly taking the deal. 

### Fight Night 
Hazelpuff frequently fights in the basement of the North Look in [[Bryn Shander]]. The same night she met the party she displayed her skill in the ring. The party gets introduced to some shady merchants wearing a symbol of a silver winged serpent.
