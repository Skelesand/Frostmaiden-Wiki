
To the Esteemed Council of the Arcane Brotherhood,

I write to you with no small measure of unease, for recent events in Icewind Dale have raised troubling questions regarding the conduct of our colleague, Avarice of the Black Quill. I must stress that what follows is, at present, speculative, but the pattern emerging is difficult to ignore.

Some weeks ago, I was contacted by Orrin Farzassh, a reclusive scholar of our organization residing in Easthaven. His message was urgent; he claimed to have received a vision from the spirits. One that he believed held dire implications for the North and required more of the Brotherhood’s attention. I set out to meet him personally. Before I could arrive, Orrin was arrested, tried, and executed for heresy under highly irregular circumstances.

Avarice’s stone gargoyles were witnessed in Easthaven on the very day of the trial. Those same constructs were sighted days later during the Drawing of the Moons, when my name was inexplicably chosen for the sacrifice. 

I cannot prove interference. Yet wherever her gargoyles appear, so too follow sudden shifts of fortune. Whether coincidence or orchestration, I leave to your judgment. But I fear Avarice is not merely observing events.

I travel now toward Caer Dineval with a small company, intent on finding something—anything—that might confirm or dispel these suspicions. I will forward my findings as soon as I—

[At the bottom of the parchment, a few fresh words bleed through the page in a darker shade of ink:]

Speculation can be dangerous, Professor.
