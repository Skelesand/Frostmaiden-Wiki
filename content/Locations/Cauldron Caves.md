A system of caves and tunnels over a volcanic line that caused natural hot springs. [[The Rime]] has gotten so cold that it has entirely frozen over and is no longer used by [[Frost Giants]].

Long ago this was a sacred burial ground of sorts for [[Frost Giants]]. When their bodies began to weaken they saw death as a more suitable alternative to weakness, and would get to embrace warmth in their final moments by drowning in the natural formed cauldron caves.

More recently [[Madam Harkle]] has moved in and harnessed the ripe powers of death that lurk within these caves. [[Arctic Winds]] and [[Lightfoot]] came in to put an end to her madness and AW was granted the strength of the [[Frost Giants]].
