## Introduction ##

Population: 600

Leader: Unknown

Termalaine was widely regarded as the most picturesque town in Icewind Dale, spreading out from the shore of Maer Dualdon and bordered on the north and west by tall pines.

For most visitors, the enchantment of the town is dispelled as soon as they feel the icy claws and teeth of the cold wind sweeping down from the north, shearing through their layers of clothing. Termalaine was built in the path of this dreadful gale, which continues well past the town to harry fishers on the southern half of the lake.

The buildings here see little to no maintenance. Many of them have collapsed in on themselves. The only public facing shop open is a small tavern called The Blue Clam.

The people here in general despise spirits and want little to do with them anymore than they already have to.

## Locations in Termalaine ##

### The Blue Clam
*Tavern*

The Blue Clam is a struggling little tavern that is run by [[Tusk]]. It seems to primarily be a place for a few of the locals to come and drink away their cold and sadness. The food here is made from very poor ingredients but magically enhanced to be delicious by Tusk.

[[Acorn Hazelpuff]] has been offered to stay here whenever she may need.

## Major Events ##

### The Druid of Lonelywood
A few weeks before the party's arrival, [[Jennings]] wandered into the woods, hobbling on his peg leg and listening with his one good ear. Soon after he had returned with both his legs and ears, along with a singular message: Everyone must learn to heal their pain from [[Celeste|The Druid of Lonelywood]].
