## Introduction ##
Population: 1,200

Leader: [[Townspeaker]] [[Duvessa Shane]]

The first stop for most visitors to [[Icewind Dale]] is Bryn Shander, a walled town perched atop a cold, lonely, wind-lashed hill. Bright lanterns suspended over narrow streets twist in the wind and add flecks of color to the town's otherwise drab surroundings.

The friendliness in this settlement has dwindled of late. [[Frostmaiden|Auril]]'s [[The Rime|unyielding winter]] has greatly reduced the number of visitors to Bryn Shander, and local trade is suffering for it, eating away at the locals' sense of humor and goodwill. Still, there is no safer place in Icewind Dale to spend coin or spend the night.

## Locations in Bryn Shander ##

### The Northlook
*Inn and Tavern*

The Northlook is the establishment most frequented by mercenaries and adventurers, and as such it's the rowdiest and most dangerous place to stay in Bryn Shander. At the same time, its taproom is the best spot in all of Ten-Towns to get leads on profitable ventures, along with the latest news and rumors.

  
The inn's proprietor, a retired sellsword ratfolk named [[Scram]], knows full well the cycles of an adventurer's life.

An underground fighting ring is hosted in the basement of this tavern. The new runners of this event also have [[Black Market]] connections.

## Major Events ##

### The Battle of Bryn Shander ###
After the first year each of the [[Townspeaker|Townspeakers]] were meant to meet in Bryn Shander to organize and take action about [[The Rime]]. What exactly went down is unclear, but during this meeting the townspeaker of [[Easthaven]] was killed and civil war erupted. A bloody month-long battle, referred to as “The Battle of Bryn Shander” or “The Red Month” ravaged the streets of Bryn Shander with 2 alliances forming: East and West. 

After a month of bloodshed and brutal losses from both sides a truce was drawn between the sides of [[Ten-Towns]] and a new organization emerged, tasked with ensuring each town received the necessary resources; This organization is called “[[The Bridges of Trust]]”. Ever since the Red Month each town has been more isolated than ever before.
