Clive is a badgerfolk from [[Easthaven]]. He owned many of the boats on the docks of Easthaven and made his business by renting them out to fisherman and adventurers.

Since [[The Rime]] Clive's business has been struggling and he has fallen deep into his mug. He continued to send fisherman out on expeditions and more often than not those expeditions stopped returning home. Clive had no choice but to continue them as the people of Easthaven needed these fish to eat, but now there are few capable or willing to embark on one of his expeditions.

The last fishing expedition that Clive sent out included [[River]] (whose father, [[Six]], would eventually come seeking help for anyone to go after [[Clover]], who ventured off into the wilderness when [[River]] hadn't returned.)

Clive and [[Arctic Winds]] do not get along.
