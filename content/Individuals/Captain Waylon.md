Dannith Waylon is a white and gray harengon that serves as the [[Townspeaker]] of [[Easthaven]].
She possesses the abilities of a paladin and can use these to detect the presence of [[chardalyn]].

Waylon comes from a militaristic background and served as a captain for the East during [[The Red Month]]. After the treaty was signed and the [[Bridges of Trust|"bridges"]] were built, Waylon returned to [[Easthaven]] to be elected as townspeaker.

She has clearly grown desperate in the last year of the [[Rime]]. She has begun performing ritual lottery style sacrifices each month to the [[Frostmaiden]] to plea for the towns protection and show their devotion.
In this brutality Waylon has found a way to give the people warmth and hope, and so she continues the ritual for as long as it can maintain the peace.
