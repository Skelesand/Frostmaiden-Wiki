Dandylin Greywhistle
A squirrelfolk professor and member of the [[Arcane Brotherhood]].

Greywhistle has been researching spirits in the north when she was called to meet in [[Easthaven]] by her colleague [[Orrin Farzassh]].

Before being kidnapped by (allegedly) one of [[Avarice]]'s [[Gargoyles]], she left behind a [[Greywhistle's Letter|letter]].

A portrait of Greywhistle was given as a gift to [[Lightfoot]] from [[Alexander]]. Alexander could not disclose who it was that gave him this painting but insisted that Lightfoot take it. Later the same day, Lightfoot felt distress coming from the painting during one of his communes with the world around him.
