Charcoal is a dire wolf that was found by [[Arctic Winds]] and [[Lightfoot]], alone and starving in the [[Cauldron Caves]]. [[Arctic Winds]] was able to tame her and help her back to health.

She accompanied the party through most of [[Chapter 1]] and served as AW's loyal friend. When [[Arctic Winds]] was killed by [[Durth Sunblight]], Charcoal was the one to deliver the killing blow to Durth. 

Now Charcoal lives on [[Kelvin's Cairn]], where she guards [[Arctic Winds]]' grave, and the Giant Runes buried with her.
