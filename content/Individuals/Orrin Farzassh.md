Orrin was a member of the [[Arcane Brotherhood]].

Orrin was burned at the stake in [[Easthaven]] for the murder of the 4 individuals who had guided him on his expedition through the Dale.

[[Professor Greywhistle]] suspects that a colleague by the name of [[Avarice]] has something to do with Orrin's death.

[[Professor Greywhistle]] was on her way to meet Orrin when she saw him burned at the stake.
