Alexander is a walking corpse that wields [[The General]]. Using this blade he commands the [[Knights of the Black Blade]] by raising those who die alone in the cold of [[Icewind Dale]] back as his undead.
He was once working with [[Lord Kadroth]], who had allowed the knights to stay in his keep, but Alexander worked with the party to kill [[Lord Kadroth]] who had been poisoning the [[Townspeaker]] of [[Caer-Dineval]], leaving Alexander as the sole leader of the knights.
Alexander has the ability to form magical [[Pact|pacts]] with people, indicating he must be some sort of [[spirit]].

## Deals Made
### Duel for the knife
Alexander and [[Arctic Winds]] would have a formal duel, the winner would get ownership of [[The General]].

### Info for Action
[[Arctic Winds]] promises to kill [[Durth Sunblight]] for Alexander, in exchange Alexander explains what exactly [[The General]] and [[The Gourmand]] are.

### A Sign of Trust
[[Lightfoot]] made a deal so that Alexander must do his best to keep the [[The Gourmand]] out of the wrong hands, in exchange Lightfoot told Alexander where the butcher's knife was (unknowingly already in Alexander's possession). Alexander is now the owner of [[The Gourmand]].
