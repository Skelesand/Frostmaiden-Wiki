## Introduction
**Auril, The Frostmaiden**
An old and powerful [[spirit]], regarded as a God by many. Some call her the source of cold itself, others accredit her with the creation of winter. It is said the ones who wander off in [[Icewind Dale]] to not return, were snatched up in Auril's talons.

Many claim it is her they see when they have a brush with death in [[Icewind Dale]].

## The Rime
She is responsible for [[The Rime]] affecting [[Icewind Dale]]. Not much is known about her motivation. Each of the [[Ten-Towns]] finds their own way to try and appease this wrathful god. Some leave out bounties of food, or extinguish flames for a full night. The most extreme offering the party has seen to the Frostmaiden was in [[Easthaven]] when they attempted to sacrifice [[Professor Greywhistle]].

## Followers
Auril's followers have dwindled in [[Ten-Towns]] since the rime began. Many now curse her (under their breath of course for fear she may actually hear it). Her most devoted followers are a circle of druids known as the [[Hailbriar Coven]].
