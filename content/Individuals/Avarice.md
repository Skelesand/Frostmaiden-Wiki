A member of the [[Arcane Brotherhood]].
[[Professor Greywhistle]] suspects that Avarice has been meddling in both her and [[Orrin Farzassh]]'s affairs.

Avarice introduced herself to [[Acorn Hazelpuff]] as an artist interested in presenting the "interesting" and "uncivilized" nature of the [[Ten-Towns]]. She left Acorn with a stone ear and the offer of gold in exchange for anything interesting that Acorn hears around town.
