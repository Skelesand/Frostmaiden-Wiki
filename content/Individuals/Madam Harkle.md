Madam Harkle had set up her base of operations in the [[Cauldron Caves]] and taken full advantage of everything the [[Frost Giants]] had left behind, including their remains.

She used their [[Cauldron of Plenty]] to cook the poor souls who wandered into her cave in search of laughter.
