### One More Deal for the Road
[[Lightfoot]] and [[Volkin Frostmere]] arrive in [[Caer-Dineval]] along with [[Kind in Autumn|Atlas]]. [[Alexander]] welcomes home the party and awaits the story of what happened. The topic of what happened to [[The Gourmand]] quickly arises. 

[[Alexander]] makes a deal with [[Lightfoot]]: He will promise to keep the knife safe, and Lightfoot will tell Alexander where to find the [[chardalyn]] knife.

As a gesture of good will, Alexander also gifts Lightfoot with a painting of [[Professor Greywhistle]] that he recently received from an unnamed benefactor.

[[Volkin Frostmere]] sensed spirits within the paintings hung in [[Caer-Dineval]].

### Knit Together
[[Acorn Hazelpuff]] is invited to meet [[Avarice]] in The Northlook (in [[Bryn Shander]]). Avarice requests that Acorn work for her as ears to the ground and offers to pay for each bit of interesting news that Acorn reports.

Acorn refuses this deal and returns to chasing the bottom of her drinks. Later on in the evening [[Lightfoot]], [[Volkin Frostmere]], and [[Nildar Sunblight]] arrive in the tavern, catching Acorns attention and endless stories.

Acorn would leave the tavern only to return even later into the evening, prepared to brawl in the fight night taking place in the basement of the Northlook.

Throughout the evening the party would begin to hear rumors circulating about town, some of which contained an air of truth. (see Rumors at the bottom of the [[index|Home Page]])

### Black Market Connections
The party connected with some shady merchants ([[Black Market]]) after [[Acorn Hazelpuff|Acorn's]] fight. After showing the wares they were interested in selling, the party received a silver whistle, which when sounded will call their fence, [[Echo]] to them.

[[Lightfoot]] and [[Volkin Frostmere]] summon [[Echo]] to sell off [[Lord Kadroth]]'s jewels and jewelry. They purchase the staff of the rooted hills, horn of silent alarm, and a pot of glowrune pigment.

Lightfoot returns to the inn only to find that [[Nildar Sunblight|Nildar]] searched through all of his things and hadn't even attempted to hide it. Nildar reveals he was searching for [[The Gourmand]] and is frustrated to hear it was left back in [[Caer-Dineval]].

### Breakfast at Hazelpuff's
Hazelpuff has arranged a nice breakfast for her new friends. They share stories and ask questions back and forth for quite some time. During this time they discuss Avarice, Madam Harkle, The Hailbriar Coven, [[Volkin Frostmere]]'s father, and [[Acorn Hazelpuff]]'s past.

The group has come to a consensus that [[Avarice]] is bad news. What exactly she wants is still unknown. Lightfoot presented the painting of [[Professor Greywhistle]] which he believes has some sort of magical connection to Greywhistle.

When [[Madam Harkle]] is mentioned [[Acorn Hazelpuff|Acorn]] can't help but react and eventually tells the party that she had made a pact with the hag long ago. While the legends go that Acorn is a mighty hag slayer, in actuality she had made a bargain for strength and extended life in exchange for leaving the hag alone. Since [[Lightfoot]] has killed the hag, Acorn has felt free again, but she has also begun to properly feel her age.

Discussing the hag naturally led the party to discussing the [[Hailbriar Coven]]. Hazelpuff revealed that the Hailbriars are a circle of druids in [[Icewind Dale]], some consider them protectors of the land, others view them as wrathful servants of The [[Frostmaiden]]. What is known for certain is that they are loyal worshipers of the [[Frostmaiden]].

Grandma Hazelpuff also reveals that she knew of a paladin back in her adventuring days that looked just like [[Volkin Frostmere]], presumably his father.

### On the road again
Before departing, [[Lightfoot]] has the town wizard try and identify the orb he had retrieved from [[Avarice]]'s expedition crew sent up [[Kelvin's Cairn]]. The wizard ([[Alakazar]]) begins the ritual, but upon learning a key bit of information refunds the gold and quickly forces Lightfoot out saying he wants nothing to do with "that person".

The party heads west to [[Targos]]. Here they see what was once a thriving fishing town is now in decay and riddled with frostbite. [[Acorn Hazelpuff]] shares some of her knitting with strangers.

They continue north towards [[Termalaine]]. Before they arrive, [[Lightfoot]] spots something hiding in the snow just before a small bridge. He launches an arrow at the creature and a fight ensues with the [[Frost Monsters|frost ogre]] and [[Frost Monsters|ice mephits]]. [[Acorn Hazelpuff|Acorn]] was knocked unconscious but Lightfoot was able to heal her wounds.

The party presses on towards [[Termalaine]].
