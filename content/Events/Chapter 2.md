### One More Deal for the Road
[[Lightfoot]] and [[Volkin Frostmere]] arrive in [[Caer-Dineval]] along with [[Kind in Autumn|Atlas]]. [[Alexander]] welcomes home the party and awaits the story of what happened. The topic of what happened to [[The Gourmand]] quickly arises. 

[[Alexander]] makes a deal with [[Lightfoot]]: He will promise to keep the knife safe, and Lightfoot will tell Alexander where to find the [[chardalyn]] knife.

As a gesture of good will, Alexander also gifts Lightfoot with a painting of [[Professor Greywhistle]] that he recently received from an unnamed benefactor.

[[Volkin Frostmere]] sensed spirits within the paintings hung in [[Caer-Dineval]].

### Knit Together
[[Acorn Hazelpuff]] is invited to meet [[Avarice]] in The Northlook (in [[Bryn Shander]]). Avarice requests that Acorn work for her as ears to the ground and offers to pay for each bit of interesting news that Acorn reports.

Acorn refuses this deal and returns to chasing the bottom of her drinks. Later on in the evening [[Lightfoot]], [[Volkin Frostmere]], and [[Nildar Sunblight]] arrive in the tavern, catching Acorns attention and endless stories.

Acorn would leave the tavern only to return even later into the evening, prepared to brawl in the fight night taking place in the basement of the Northlook.

Throughout the evening the party would begin to hear rumors circulating about town, some of which contained an air of truth. (see Rumors at the bottom of the [[index|Home Page]])

### Black Market Connections
The party connected with some shady merchants after [[Acorn Hazelpuff|Acorn's]] fight. After showing the wares they were interested in selling, the party received a silver whistle, which when sounded will call their fence, [[Echo]] to them.

[[Lightfoot]] and [[Volkin Frostmere]] summon [[Echo]] to sell off [[Lord Kadroth]]'s jewels and jewelry, and possibly by some of Echo's wares.
