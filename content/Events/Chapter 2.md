### One More Deal for the Road
[[Lightfoot]] and [[Volkin Frostmere]] arrive in [[Caer-Dineval]] along with [[Kind in Autumn|Atlas]]. [[Alexander]] welcomes home the party and awaits the story of what happened. The topic of what happened to [[The Gourmand]] quickly arises. 

[[Alexander]] makes a deal with [[Lightfoot]]: He will promise to keep the knife safe, and Lightfoot will tell Alexander where to find the [[chardalyn]] knife.

As a gesture of good will, Alexander also gifts Lightfoot with a painting of [[Professor Greywhistle]] that he recently received from an unnamed benefactor.

[[Volkin Frostmere]] sensed spirits within the paintings hung in [[Caer-Dineval]].

### Knit Together
[[Acorn Hazelpuff]] is invited to meet [[Avarice]] in The Northlook (in [[Bryn Shander]]). Avarice requests that Acorn work for her as ears to the ground and offers to pay for each bit of interesting news that Acorn reports.

Acorn refuses this deal and returns to chasing the bottom of her drinks. Later on in the evening [[Lightfoot]], [[Volkin Frostmere]], and [[Nildar Sunblight]] arrive in the tavern, catching Acorns attention and endless stories.

Acorn would leave the tavern only to return even later into the evening, prepared to brawl in the fight night taking place in the basement of the Northlook.

Throughout the evening the party would begin to hear rumors circulating about town, some of which contained an air of truth. (see Rumors at the bottom of the [[index|Home Page]])

### Black Market Connections
The party connected with some shady merchants ([[Black Market]]) after [[Acorn Hazelpuff|Acorn's]] fight. After showing the wares they were interested in selling, the party received a silver whistle, which when sounded will call their fence, [[Echo]] to them.

[[Lightfoot]] and [[Volkin Frostmere]] summon [[Echo]] to sell off [[Lord Kadroth]]'s jewels and jewelry. They purchase the staff of the rooted hills, horn of silent alarm, and a pot of glowrune pigment.

Lightfoot returns to the inn only to find that [[Nildar Sunblight|Nildar]] searched through all of his things and hadn't even attempted to hide it. Nildar reveals he was searching for [[The Gourmand]] and is frustrated to hear it was left back in [[Caer-Dineval]].

### Breakfast at Hazelpuff's
Hazelpuff has arranged a nice breakfast for her new friends. They share stories and ask questions back and forth for quite some time. During this time they discuss Avarice, Madam Harkle, The Hailbriar Coven, [[Volkin Frostmere]]'s father, and [[Acorn Hazelpuff]]'s past.

The group has come to a consensus that [[Avarice]] is bad news. What exactly she wants is still unknown. Lightfoot presented the painting of [[Professor Greywhistle]] which he believes has some sort of magical connection to Greywhistle.

When [[Madam Harkle]] is mentioned [[Acorn Hazelpuff|Acorn]] can't help but react and eventually tells the party that she had made a pact with the hag long ago. While the legends go that Acorn is a mighty hag slayer, in actuality she had made a bargain for strength and extended life in exchange for leaving the hag alone. Since [[Lightfoot]] has killed the hag, Acorn has felt free again, but she has also begun to properly feel her age.

Discussing the hag naturally led the party to discussing the [[Hailbriar Coven]]. Hazelpuff revealed that the Hailbriars are a circle of druids in [[Icewind Dale]], some consider them protectors of the land, others view them as wrathful servants of The [[Frostmaiden]]. What is known for certain is that they are loyal worshipers of the [[Frostmaiden]].

Grandma Hazelpuff also reveals that she knew of a paladin back in her adventuring days that looked just like [[Volkin Frostmere]], presumably his father.

### On the road again
Before departing, [[Lightfoot]] has the town wizard try and identify the orb he had retrieved from [[Avarice]]'s expedition crew sent up [[Kelvin's Cairn]]. The wizard ([[Alakazar]]) begins the ritual, but upon learning a key bit of information refunds the gold and quickly forces Lightfoot out saying he wants nothing to do with "that person".

The party heads west to [[Targos]]. Here they see what was once a thriving fishing town is now in decay and riddled with frostbite. [[Acorn Hazelpuff]] shares some of her knitting with strangers.

They continue north towards [[Termalaine]]. Before they arrive, [[Lightfoot]] spots something hiding in the snow just before a small bridge. He launches an arrow at the creature and a fight ensues with the [[Frost Monsters|frost ogre]] and [[Frost Monsters|ice mephits]]. [[Acorn Hazelpuff|Acorn]] was knocked unconscious but Lightfoot was able to heal her wounds. The party presses on towards [[Termalaine]].

### Sweet Dreams
[[Rie]] arrived in [[Termalaine]] the day before the party did. She couldn't find a place to stay, a warm meal, or even a soul to talk to, so she found a place to take cover from the wind and fell asleep in the alley. (sleeping outside is a feat few could survive)

[[Rie]] experienced a vivid dream. She was deep beneath the surface of the water. Looking up she could see what looked like a starry night sky on the water's surface. There was a silver koi fish and a golden whale swimming opposite each other in a large circle. She suddenly awoke when something snatched the koi fish from the water.

### The Mad Rabbit
[[Jennings]], a crazed rabbitfolk, spotted [[Rie]] and began raving about [[Celeste|The Druid of Lonelywood]], her lessons from the cold, and how she can save you from your pain. The party soon stumbles upon this scene as they arrive into town and join in on the questioning. [[Jennings]] latches onto [[Acorn Hazelpuff]], as she is  visibly injured and he wants the Druid to save her.

[[Nildar Sunblight|Nildar]] remarks that the druid he met with in [[Lonelywood]] was named [[Celeste]] and is "almost certainly" the same druid that Jennings is talking about.

### The Blue Clam
[[Tusk]] welcomes everyone to [[Termalaine|The Blue Clam]]. We get to known [[Rie]] better and she is quickly welcomed into the party (signified by [[Acorn Hazelpuff|Acorn's]] knit flower in [[Rie|Rie's]] hat). Everyone shares their story to varying degrees and get to know one another over some (magically) delicious and cheap food.

[[Tusk]] tells the party about how [[Jennings]] wasn't always crazy like that, but ever since he messed around with spirits he lost his mind. He also warns the party they shouldn't go into those woods, as there's a wraith that haunts them.

[[Acorn Hazelpuff|Acorn]] mentions the Battle of [[Bryn Shander]] which quickly upsets [[Tusk]] and quiets the room. Later after confirming that Acorn and Tusk fought on the same side of the battle, Acorn is offered a place to stay whenever she needs.

[[Nildar Sunblight|Nildar]] is eager to head into the woods and seems to think he deserves to be recognized as a leader or at least a form of authority.

### Into the woods
The party searches for many hours, spending all of their twilight lost in the woods. During their search they discover this to be a sort of haven for wildlife (much like [[Kelvin's Cairn]])

There are wails of pain heard on the wind, whose source cant be placed for quite a long time. Eventually [[Acorn Hazelpuff|Hazelpuff]] figures the townsfolk were mistaken in calling the creature of these woods a wraith, and what they truly meant was a [[Banshee]]. A creature whose scream can stop a beating heart.

The party rushes as fast as they can out of the forest but with darkness taking hold of the woods, the [[Banshee]] confronts the party at last and stops Acorn's heart. As the banshee's form can finally be plainly seen, it is black ghostly fox-folk, with a bow in hand and a large open wound across his chest. [[Lightfoot]] recognizes the weapon responsible for this wound: a scythe, once wielded by his friend [[Arctic Winds]].

The party defeats the banishes the banshee, but [[Lightfoot]] guesses it'll only be a week or so before it reforms. [[Nildar Sunblight]] wasted no time in running away and now was nowhere to be found. While resting and recovering from their fight, [[Volkin Frostmere]] felt his sword pulling him in a specific direction. The party follows Volkin's sword like a compass and are lead to the [[Sanctuary of the Moon]].

### Moon Magic
After following [[Volkin Frostmere|Volkin's]] sword for some time the party arrived at the [[Sanctuary of the Moon]]. No one else was there so the party did some investigating around the giant moon dial and chambers inside. They discovered:
- The moon dial absorbs moonlight and projects it onto the current phase of the moon inscribed in the ground.
- There is some sort of ritual which requires a stick, feather, pine cone, and willing severed hand be tossed into a brazier.
- 5 crystal pillars are each marked with a symbol, recognized to be depicting the tools of [[The Ebon Council]]:
	- Staff (Unknown)
	- Net (Possibly [[The Hunter]])
	- Knife ([[The Gourmand]])
	- Sword ([[The General]])
	- Mortar & Pestle ([[The Ritualmaster]])
- The doors inside are magically locked to phases of the moon
- There is a magic scrying mirror
- There are many statues of caribou-folk lining the edges of the sanctuary

[[Acorn Hazelpuff]] bends the tip of the moon dial to "speed up time" and get to the quarter moon to open up the chamber that [[Volkin Frostmere|Volkin's]] sword is pulling him towards. Inside there is a ritual table with an inlay to perfectly fit the Frostmere family blade. Placing the sword into the ritual table causes it to fill with shimmering liquid moonlight, enchanting the sword and binding it to Volkin.

### Celeste, of the Stars

As the party heads back outside from performing this ritual, they are met by [[Celeste]], who was quietly observing them before [[Acorn Hazelpuff|Acorn]] attempted to bend the moon dial again and [[Rie]] noticed the massive snow white dire wolves, motionlessly standing guard at each possible exit.

[[Celeste]] introduces herself as a healer of [[Lonelywood]] and a druid that is more fond of the stars than the land. [[Celeste]] offers healing to the party and [[Acorn Hazelpuff|Acorn]] accepts. She also explains that she is a member of the [[Hailbriar Coven]] and they are direct servants of the [[Frostmaiden]]. According to Celeste, violent acts from the coven are merely retribution for what the people of [[Ten-Towns]] do to the land and lakes. When questioned about [[The Rime]] she says that this is not the first time the Frostmaiden has brought about an ice age, and when she has done that in the past it was to keep something much worse dormant. She also reveals that the only way to get to this place is by already knowing where it is (much like the [[Spirit World]]). The caribou-folk constructed these statues to keep the sanctuary safe from outsiders. 

If the ritual in the brazier is performed correctly the willingly given hand will be turned into a snow owl familiar.

During all of this discussion, [[Celeste]] refers to herself as master of rituals and on multiple occasions underestimates or disrespects druidic magic, revealing she is likely not who she says she is.

The whole group decide they need to get a bit of rest from the cold winds so they head inside (where [[Volkin Frostmere]] had been keeping to himself with his newly enchanted sword and the feeling of his father's spirit). Immediately upon seeing [[Volkin Frostmere|Volkin]], Celeste utters the name "Frostmere" and is immediately hostile. Combat breaks out.

[[Celeste]] conjures a sleet storm inside, which blinds everyone, makes it extremely difficult to move, and constantly threatens to break concentration on spells. [[Rie]] does not let this last for long as she uses Dispel Magic to dismiss this storm and begins to swing the fight in their favor. This also reveals that Celeste was making a run for the Scrying Mirror

[[Lightfoot]] is ripped limb from limb in his spiderform but is able to wildshape once more and has a moonbeam protecting the entrance inside

[[Acorn Hazelpuff]] uses her ancestral guardians to protect [[Rie]] and [[Lightfoot]] from the wolves while dishing out insane damage (I really don't know if she got below a 20 to hit lol)

[[Volkin Frostmere]] cast bless on the party (clutching quite a few saving throws) and runs down [[Celeste]] before she can escape, throwing a (nat 20) dagger into her back just before she can reach the mirror

[[Rie]] takes the brunt of the attacks as her spirit guardians deal massive damage but paint a large target on her back

[[Rie]] was knocked unconscious but brought back by [[Lightfoot]].

As [[Celeste]] comes back to, she tries to escape. When that doesn't work she summons the [[The Ritualmaster|mortar & pestle]] to her hand and begins to perform a twisted desperate act of magic. The party acts quick to sever her hand holding the mortar. It is a grim sight. She does not react to the pain and in her last moments was desperately pouring her own blood into the Mortar as it gets soaked into the artifact. When she dies it is sudden, her body drops lifeless as the last bit of blood is absorbed by the chardalyn.

[[Acorn Hazelpuff]], a friend of the dead, is able to cast speak with dead on [[Celeste]]. The Celeste they are speaking to seems to be an acolyte of the moon with no recollection of the artifact or who [[Volkin Frostmere]] is, all but confirming they were not speaking with Celeste earlier, but [[The Ritualmaster]] themself. Now the question is: how much of what she said can be trusted?

Nildar's journal is found on [[Celeste]], indicating she had likely found [[Nildar Sunblight|Nildar]] after he tried to flea from the [[Banshee]].

The party does some more investigating and discovers more things:
- The last door in the chamber is opened and they find the scrawling of a madman over top of a chamber that once served as some sort of ritual involving the stars. There are many papers with similar indecipherable writings and signs that whoever was in here had been staying in here for a while. Unfortunately the party cant decipher much about what was happening in this room.
- The use of metal here indicates this isn't purely a druidic site. It is a safe haven for druids, protected by an order of paladins (of which [[Volkin Frostmere|Volkin's]] father was a member).
- The party attempts to use [[Celeste]]'s severed hand in the ritual brazier, which initially begins to form an owl but quickly twists into a [[Frost Monsters|Ice Mephit]].

As the [[Frost Monsters|Ice Mephit]] is forming [[Acorn Hazelpuff|Acorn]] and [[Volkin Frostmere|Volkin]] want to immediately kill the creature, while [[Rie]] and [[Lightfoot]] insist they do not harm it until they can see its nature. [[Acorn Hazelpuff|Acorn]] pushes past them and squashes the mephit before it can fully form. This causes a tear in the party as Acorn and Volkin believed they were protecting the party from the monster, while Rie and Lightfoot believe it is wrong to attack an individual that they have not seen act.

[[Rie]] questions whether this is actually the crew for her. [[Acorn Hazelpuff|Acorn]] apologizes for acting rashly which Rie appreciates but does not accept.

With each individual looking for different things for the moment, the party decides to split up for a week and meet back in [[Bryn Shander]], at Acorn's house.
