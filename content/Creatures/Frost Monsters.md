Monsters constructed entirely of ice have been terrorizing travelers between the [[Ten-Towns]]. There are many variations of this creature: Our party has encountered goblins, ogres, and mephits. The party has attempted investigating their nature, whether they're spirits, constructs, golems, or some other thing, the party is still unsure.

[[Volkin Frostmere]] has been investigating these creatures and eventually tracked them to originate from the forest of [[Lonelywood]]. He found a troop of these creatures and tracked them all the way to [[Kelvin's Cairn]] where they arrived at the Sunblight Outpost.

[[Nildar Sunblight]] revealed that he had met with a druid in [[Lonelywood]] to arrange for these troops to be used by the [[Sunblights]]. Nildar offered the party to take them to where he went in Lonelywood in exchange for their protection.
