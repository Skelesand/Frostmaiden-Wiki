Powerful constructs made of stone. When motionless they are indistinguishable from a statue. They are especially well known for their equal amounts of stealth and strength.

Gargoyles were spotted in [[Easthaven]], following where [[Professor Greywhistle]] went. Greywhistle later commented on "[[Avarice]]'s gargoyles" being present in Easthaven in [[Greywhistle's Letter]].

One of [[Avarice]]'s gargoyles kidnapped [[Professor Greywhistle]]. This particular gargoyle seemed to be covered in some sort of white paint.
