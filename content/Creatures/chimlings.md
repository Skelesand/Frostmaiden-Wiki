Small spirits that look like pine tree saplings with masks and limbs. They jingle when they walk and are quite the little kleptos. They are incredibly fast and can pass through the veil between the material and [[Spirit World]] with ease.

One of these chimlings has befriended [[Lightfoot]] and will follow him around, or at least check in on him on occasion.

[[Professor Greywhistle]] has been studying these creatures, particularly the manner in which they can travel. She paid [[Arctic Winds]] and [[Lightfoot]] for the field research they accomplished for her and supplied Lightfoot with a [[Lantern of Spirit Finding]] (which she had intended on upgrading for Lightfoot and returning, but other things got in the way).
