An undead group of knights under the control of [[Alexander]]. Each member has a similar story: They were out in the dale when the frost finally took the last of their warmth, but just as they were losing hope a [[chardalyn]] amulet presents itself, and the cold feeling goes away. This process turns them into an undead under Alexander's control thanks to his magic longsword, [[The General]].

The Knights live in [[Caer-Dineval|Castle Dineval]] where they have been trying to gather numbers. Recently they suffered heavy losses at the hands of the [[Sunblights]], led by [[Durth Sunblight]]. 

The Knights follow Alexander's sense of vengeance and justice, often times to serve the residents of [[Ten-Towns]] but rarely with any regard for others.

Alexander has chosen [[Kind in Autumn]] as his apprentice, and given him the new name: Atlas.
