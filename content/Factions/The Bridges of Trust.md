## Foundation
After the first year of [[The Rime]] each of the [[Townspeaker|Townspeakers]] were meant to meet in Bryn Shander to organize and take action about [[The Rime]]. What exactly went down is unclear, but during this meeting the townspeaker of [[Easthaven]] was killed and civil war erupted. A bloody month-long battle, referred to as “The Battle of [[Bryn Shander]]” or “The Red Month” ravaged the streets of Bryn Shander with 2 alliances forming: East and West. 

After a month of bloodshed and brutal losses from both sides a truce was drawn between the sides of [[Ten-Towns]] and a new organization emerged, tasked with ensuring each town received the necessary resources; This organization is called “[[The Bridges of Trust]]”. Ever since the Red Month each town has been more isolated than ever before.

This organization serves to distribute resources from the larger cities like Bryn Shander and Targos to the smaller towns. They are certainly fighting an uphill battle.
