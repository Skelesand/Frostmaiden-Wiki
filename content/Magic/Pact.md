A pact is a magical deal or bargain that is struck with a [[spirit]] and are typically bound until death or completion of a task. Not just any spirit can create a pact; they must be quite powerful if they can form one.

If a pact is broken there are always consequences.

### Known Pacts ###
A magical contract between "Mr.Ages" and [[Lord Kadroth]] was found in his desk.

[[Alexander]]'s [[chardalyn]] amulets function as a sort of pact.

[[Alexander]] made two pacts with [[Arctic Winds]]; One to make a bet, the winner receiving [[The Gourmand]], and the other an exchange of information on [[The General]] for the murder of [[Durth Sunblight]].
