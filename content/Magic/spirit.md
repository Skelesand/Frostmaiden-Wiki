A spirit is a very loose term that encompasses all things other worldly. A spirit is always a representation of something in the material world. For example a ruthless goblin is a monstrous spirit that can be conjured through a ritual of bloodshed. On the other hand, there are powerful spirits that represent broader concepts like warmth, joy, or even revenge.

Our party has seen [[chimlings]], [[Hunger Spirits]], [[guardian spirits]], frost spirits, and various forms of magic that can be channeled from spirits.
