An ancient mortar & pestle made from [[chardalyn]]. Its creator infused themselves and their magic into this tool, and all that was left of them is their title; they remained: "The Ritualmaster".

Listed as apart of [[The Ebon Council]].

**Previous Owner: [[Celeste]]
Current Owner: [[Lightfoot]]

## Abilities
This artifact had dominated the mind of the druid [[Celeste]].
The specific abilities of this relic are unknown. [[Celeste]] was seen casting healing magic from it and in an act of desperation, some sort of spell involving her own blood.

It can be summoned and dismissed at will by its user.
