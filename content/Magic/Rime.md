A spell woven over the entirety of Icewind Dale by The [[Frostmaiden]]. This spell causes the entire climate to continue to get colder and colder and blocks out the sun from ever shining, eventually turning the entire place into an uninhabitable wasteland if not stopped in time.

The rime has been going for 2 years and has dwindled the population of [[Ten-Towns]] and the surrounding inhabitants of [[Icewind Dale]].

Some have found great opportunity in the Rime, using the enhanced powers of ice and snow for their own machinations, or some creatures that lurked in the dark below have now found opportunity in a sunless surface (see [[Sunblights]]).
